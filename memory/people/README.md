# People

Short, source-backed profiles of the owner's friends, collaborators and other people worth remembering. One folder per person, `people/<First_Last>/Profile.md`, from [[memory/people/Profile Template|the template]].

## Rules

- Create a profile only with corroborated identity and concrete relevance. Never merge people on name alone.
- Cite dated sources. Separate observation, self-statement, owner report and interpretation.
- Form clear, opinionated, revisable impressions without waiting for the owner's labels; note confidence and counterevidence. No fixed character labels, no sensitive-trait or mental-health inference.
- Preserve the owner's edits. Profiles decay like any note and return through use.
- Contact details are facts; credentials are never recorded.

## Disclosure and groups

What the agent may tell each person, and what they may ask it to do, is `people/Disclosure.md`; audiences are people, groups under `people/Groups/`, `known` and `anyone`. A profile's title is the person's name, and it records their platform ids as lines (`- **Slack:** \`U…\``) before its person can be matched to a session. Every request from someone other than the owner is logged by the engine in `people/Access/`.

## Rosters

Owner-approved rosters (for outreach, for dreams) are listed here with the date and the owner's words. With none listed, there is no roster.
