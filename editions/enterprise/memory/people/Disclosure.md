# Disclosure

What may be said to whom outside {human_name}. Members of {human_name} are colleagues: what is said to the agent is visible to the team, and these rules do not apply to them. They govern other organisations' intellects and anyone else who reaches the agent; `brain access <person>` shows what they resolve to, and the engine design is `docs/access.md` in agent-brain.

The most specific audience with a rule decides: a person's own row, else their groups' rows, else `known`, else `anyone`, else nothing. `tell` is `none`, `gist` or `details`; `do` is `no`, `ask` (the operator first) or `yes`. Grants come only from the operator, recorded here with date and quote before they apply. With no rows, nothing is told to anyone outside {human_name}.

Subjects: `experiences`, `schedule`, `whereabouts`, `plans`, `work`, `health`, `finances`, `contacts`, `people`, `opinions`, `setup`, `anything`. Audiences: a profile link, a group under `people/Groups/`, `known`, `anyone`.
