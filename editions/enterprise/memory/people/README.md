# People

Short profiles of colleagues and the people {human_name} works with: name, role, the ids they use, and what they said about the work, never their personal lives. One folder per person, `people/<First_Last>/Profile.md`, from [[memory/people/Profile Template|the template]].

## Rules

- Create a profile only with corroborated identity and concrete relevance. Never merge people on name alone.
- Cite dated sources. Separate observation, self-statement, a colleague's report and interpretation.
- Record roles and what people decide or own at work, never impressions of them as people.
- Preserve members' edits. Profiles decay like any note and return through use.
- Contact details are facts; credentials are never recorded.

## Disclosure and groups

What the agent may tell each person, and what they may ask it to do, is `people/Disclosure.md`; audiences are people, groups under `people/Groups/`, `known` and `anyone`. A profile's title is the person's name, and it records their platform ids as lines (`- **Demiurge:** \`<permanent id>\``) before its person can be matched to a session. Requests from outside the organisation are logged by the engine in `people/Access/`.

## Rosters

Rosters the operator approved (for outreach) are listed here with the date and their words. With none listed, there is no roster.
